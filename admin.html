How sellers join

  1. They apply: share gdxgarage.com/admin.html → Continue with Google → short form. Applications appear at the top of this tab — tap Approve or Reject.
  2. Or you add them: + Add seller → shop name and their Gmail address → Save. Their shop opens the first time they sign in with that Google account.
  3. After approval they add their UPI ID in My shop, then they can list cars.
  4. Sellers have no passwords — they always sign in with Google. To remove access: Edit → untick Active.
