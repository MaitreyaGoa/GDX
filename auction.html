How auctions work
  1. Everyone signs in with Google. Sellers also give a WhatsApp number (shown only to the winner).
  2. Each new bid must be at least the current bid + the seller's minimum step (the first bid at least the starting bid). Lower bids are rejected automatically.
  3. Final 2 minutes: each new bid must be at least ₹100 higher (or the seller's step if bigger), and every bid resets the clock to 2 minutes. The auction ends after 2 minutes with no new bid — so last-second sniping doesn't work.
  4. You can't bid on your own auction or outbid yourself. Bids can't be withdrawn.
  5. Buy Now ends the auction immediately at that price.
  6. When it ends, the highest bidder wins. The winner and seller then see each other's contact details and arrange payment and delivery directly. GDX only hosts the auction.
